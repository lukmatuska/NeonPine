NEONPINE
Quick startNeon glow‑lamp tree · USB‑C 5 V
1
Stand it up Push the tabs at the bottom of the tree and of its rear cover into the four slots of the stand base. The side marked FRONT faces the lamps.
2
Plug it in Connect a USB‑C cable to the socket on the right of the “pot” and to a certified 5 V USB adapter or a computer.
3
Let it glow The tree lights up after about 5 s. In a completely dark room the lamps can take a few seconds to strike. This is normal.

Safety · read and keep these instructions

  • Not a toy. A decoration for adults, recommended for ages 14+. Not suitable for children under 3: it contains small parts and glass lamps. Keep it away from children and pets.
  • Indoor use only, in dry rooms. Keep it away from water and condensation (e.g. a cold windowsill).
  • Power it only from a certified 5 V USB adapter (CE / UL‑listed, IEC 62368‑1) or a computer USB port.
  • Do not remove the rear cover. Inside is a circuit of up to about 110 V DC. There are no user‑serviceable parts.
  • Do not bend, pull or press the glass lamps. If one breaks, unplug the tree, collect the glass wearing gloves, and stop using it.
  • Use the supplied stand on a stable, flat surface, away from heat sources and open flames. Route the cable where nobody trips over it and children can’t reach it.
  • Unplug it if it is damaged, makes unusual noises or smells, or does not work correctly.

Troubleshooting

Stays darkCheck cable and adapter. It turns off in daylight: cover it with your hand.
Lamps start lateNormal for neon in the dark; they light within seconds.
Flickers on a PCUse an adapter that supplies at least 0.5 A.
CareUnplug, wipe with a dry, soft cloth. No liquids.
Disposal: do not put this product in household waste. Take it to a collection point for electrical and electronic equipment; retailers of electrical equipment take it back free of charge.
