## Read and keep these instructions

### ⚠ Safety information
* **Not a toy.** This is a decoration for adults. Not suitable for children under 3 years: it contains small parts and glass
  lamps. Keep it out of the reach of children and pets. Recommended for ages 14+.
* **Indoor use only.** Use in dry rooms only. Do not place it where it can get wet or covered in condensation (e.g. a cold
  windowsill), and do not clean it with liquids.
* **Power it only from a certified 5 V USB power adapter** (marked CE / UL‑listed, compliant with IEC 62368‑1) or a computer
  USB port. A power adapter is not included.
* **Do not remove the rear cover.** The product contains an internal circuit of up to about 110 V DC. There are no
  user‑serviceable parts inside.
* **Do not bend, pull or press the glass lamps.** If a lamp breaks: unplug the product, carefully collect the glass wearing gloves,
  and stop using the product.
* Place it on a stable, flat surface using the supplied stand. Keep it away from heat sources and open flames.
* Do not leave the USB cable where children can reach it, or where people can trip over it.
* Unplug it if it is damaged, makes unusual noises or smells, or does not work correctly.

## Getting started
1. Push the two tabs at the bottom of the tree and of its rear cover into the four slots of the stand base (the side
   marked FRONT faces the lamps).
2. Connect a USB‑C cable to the socket on the right side of the "pot", and connect the other end to a 5 V USB power adapter.
3. The tree lights up. Neon lamps may take a few seconds to light the first time in a completely dark room. This is normal.

## Controls
| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Tap the **trunk pad** (round pad at the bottom left, below "NEON PINE") | Next light animation (crossfade) |
| Touch and hold the trunk pad | Adjust brightness (ramps up/down) |
| Double‑tap the trunk pad | Sleep timer on/off (dims over 20 min, then off) |
| Hold the trunk pad for 3 s | Off / on |
| Wave a hand over the tree | Next animation |
| Room gets dark / bright | Turns on at dusk, off in daylight; brightness follows the room (light sensor = the small ball in the left branch) |

Animations: Candle · Breathe · Twinkle · Garland · Snowfall · Heartbeat · Drift (changes automatically every 3 minutes) · Sleep.

## Troubleshooting
| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| Nothing lights up | Check the cable and adapter. In daylight the tree switches off automatically; cover the tree with your hand to test |
| Some lamps light up late in the dark | Normal for neon lamps; they light within seconds |
| Lamps flicker on a computer USB port | Use a USB power adapter that can supply at least 0.5 A |
| The computer reports an unknown or unrecognised USB device | Unplug it and plug it in again, or use a power adapter if you do not need the computer connection `[firmware‑specific]` |
