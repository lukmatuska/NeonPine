# NeonPine – markings, labelling & packaging

## 1. On the product (rear cover, silkscreen or a durable label)
| Marking | Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **CE** mark, ≥ 5 mm high | EMC / RoHS | Proportions per Reg. 765/2008 Annex II |
| **Crossed‑out wheeled bin** + producer ID or **date bar** | WEEE, EN 50419 | If too small, may go on packaging, manual and warranty (WEEE Art. 14(4)) |
| Model `NP‑1`, hardware rev, **batch or serial number** | GPSR Art. 9(5) | e.g. `NP-1 B 2611-0042` (rev B, year 26 week 11, serial 0042) |
| Manufacturer name / trademark + postal **and** electronic address | GPSR Art. 9(6) | May go on packaging if it doesn't fit; a web address alone is not enough |
| EU economic operator (if the manufacturer is outside the EU) | GPSR Art. 16 | |
| Rating: `5 V ⎓ 0.15 A` and the DC symbol (IEC 60417‑5031) | good practice / IEC 60598 | |
| "Indoor use only" symbol (IEC 60417‑5957) | instruction support | |
| UK importer name and address (if importing to GB) | UK GPSR 2005 | |
| FCC: model + responsible party (statement in the manual) | §15.19, §2.1077 | see 05‑fcc‑sdoc.md |
| Country of origin "Made in `[…]`" | US customs | product or packaging |

**Rev B: implemented in the rear‑cover silkscreen** (`NeonPine-cover.kicad_pcb`, generated by `tools/cover.py`, see `docs/img/cover-and-stand.png`).
The bracketed fields are placeholders in `tools/cover.py` – fill them in before ordering. The original proposal was:
```
NeonPine  NP-1  rev B          5 V ⎓ 0.15 A   [⌂ indoor]
S/N 2611-0042                  [CE]  [WEEE bin + bar]
[Company], [street], [city], [country] · [support e-mail]
Not a toy · Not for children under 14   Made in [country]
```

## 2. Packaging
* Product name, model, the manufacturer and EU operator addresses (if not legible on the product), CE, WEEE symbol (if not on the product).
* **Warnings** (in each language of sale):
  * "Not a toy. Not suitable for children under 3 years: contains small parts and glass. Keep out of reach of children." *(choose
    the exact wording with the lab; recommended age 14+)*
  * "Indoor use only. Use only with a certified USB power adapter (5 V)."
* Packaging material identification (PPWR (EU) 2025/40 as its labelling acts apply; national rules such as the French Triman/Info‑tri and
  Italian D.Lgs. 116/2020 environmental labelling).
* Prop 65 warning for California if the lead content isn't excluded by the supplier declarations.
* Barcode (GTIN) and the batch code matching the product.

## 3. Documents in the box
* Quick start + safety instructions ([../user-manual.md](../user-manual.md)) in every language of the sales country.
* FCC compliance information statement (US SKU).
* Simplified EU DoC **or** a URL to the full DoC (`[https://…/doc/NP-1]`).
