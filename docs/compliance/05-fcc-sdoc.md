* Reorient or relocate the receiving antenna.
* Increase the separation between the equipment and receiver.
* Connect the equipment into an outlet on a circuit different from that to which the receiver is connected.
* Consult the dealer or an experienced radio/TV technician for help.

## Labelling (§15.19)
* The product is small, but the board has room for the short statement on the rear cover. If it doesn't fit legibly (≥ 4–6 pt),
  §15.19(a)(5) allows it to go in the user manual or on the packaging instead. **Recommended:** the full statement in the manual
  + packaging, plus model and responsible party name on the rear cover.
* The FCC logo is optional for SDoC devices.

## SDoC record to keep (not shipped)
* Lab test report (accredited lab), test setup photos, firmware version, adapter and cable used.
* Schematic, BOM, PCB rev and photos of the production unit.
* A statement that production units are identical to the tested sample, plus a change‑control note: re‑evaluate for any change to
  the boost converter, the MCU clock, the PCB layout or the USB connector.
